A course in eight lessons

Quantum Error Correction in Practice

Eight lessons take you from the repetition code to lattice surgery. You flip qubits and watch the checks fire, step through a union-find decoder as its clusters grow, and in the threshold lesson run a Monte Carlo simulation that draws the threshold plot as it goes. A stabilizer simulator and the decoder run in the page, with nothing to install.

Tool

Bivariate bicycle code explorer

Type the two polynomials of a bivariate bicycle code and the page gives its numbers of physical and logical qubits and its check weights, draws its checks on a torus, and searches for an upper bound on its distance. A link reproduces the code, and its check matrices download as plain text.

About and contact

I am currently a Senior Software Engineer at DataRobot; before that I worked at Agnostiq as a research software engineer on gen-AI algorithms.
