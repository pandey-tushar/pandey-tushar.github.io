Topology

The volume conjecture

Ph.D. work under Tian Yang in quantum topology. A generalized Bonahon–Wong–Yang volume conjecture, proved for the figure-eight knot complement with Ka Ho Wong (Algebraic & Geometric Topology, to appear); and, sole author, the conjecture verified for four-puncture sphere bundles under technical conditions.

Interactive

Toric code playground

Each qubit is a button: Tab reaches the lattice, the arrow keys move between qubits, and Enter or Space flips one. The status line below says which checks are lit.

A distance-5 toric code: click a qubit to cause an error, the checks that light up show where the code notices it, and Correct makes the decoder’s best guess at what went wrong.
