=== CP-EQ : depth-vs-liveness Pareto frontier of the whole-F XAGs ===
Ordering only (every AND executed exactly once, no recomputation).
Model: 1 register per AND-node output, held from its production step until
its last consumer step.  "end" = the single CZ consumes all its feeders at
one instant (cpeo_dag's definition).  "incr" = the CZ accumulator absorbs
each feeder as soon as it exists (feeders with no AND consumer die at once).
Tool: cpeq_dag.py (DAG extraction) + cpeq_pareto.py (CP-SAT cumulative
scheduling, minimise makespan in AND-levels subject to capacity K).

optimal: objective value == best bound), vs the ASAP figure in cpeo_results:
  XAG                   nands  AND-depth  ASAP peak  min peak (end)  (incr)
  cpdm_wide_tree.prog     47       6         34           29           29
  cpdm_wide.prog          48       6         38           33           33
  cpdl_best.prog          41      19         27           20           20
=> ordering alone buys 5 / 5 / 7 registers; the "incr" phase model buys 0.

   quantity for these XAGs.

CONCLUSION (measured): at the register budget that exists (6 clean ancillas
during every level where these XAGs fire most of their ANDs; 18 counting
every wire in the machine as a register, which destroys the inputs) the
minimum AND-depth of every whole-F XAG in the repo is undefined -- no
ordering exists at all, for K = 6, 8, 10, 12 and (for the cpdm family) for
every K <= 28.  The first feasible budgets are 20 (cpdl_best), 29
(cpdm_wide_tree), 33 (cpdm_wide), 45 (cpdm_n47), >=14 and <=18 (cpeh_78 /
yrev_xrev ROBDD).  Nothing was built; the banked best stays
cpen_best.qasm 258 / 520.
