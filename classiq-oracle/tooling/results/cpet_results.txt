WHAT BLOCKS IT (single measured number): (B), the wire packing.
At the (A) budget T=24 the computation is INFEASIBLE at 20 wires and
OPTIMAL at 30 wires in the one-wire-per-node-value model (CP-SAT), and the
real build needs 123-368 wires to reach 127-430 depth.  (A) is not the
blocker: the chain is 8 RCCX-equivalents forward, 3x inside budget.
The 18-wire gap is NOT closed by that model, because a wire can carry an
XOR-accumulation of several node values (measured on the banked circuit:
23 live node values and 32 model-wires on 18 real wires).  The untried
lever is therefore an emitter that packs several node values per wire
as XOR accumulations, which is exactly what the banked circuit does by
hand and what every emitter in this run does not.
