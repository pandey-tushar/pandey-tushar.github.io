=== Stage 0: reading + design (done) ===
Read CP-BO, CP-CY, CP-DA, CP-CD(2), cpbo_cube.py/cpbo_shape.py,
cpcy_lib.py/cpcy_compose.py, cpda_lib.py/cpda_compose.py, cpch_oracle.py,
logo.py, verify18.py.

Key facts used:
- assert_ro (cpcy_lib) forbids ANY gate targeting q0-11 -- checked on the
  TARGET only.  A phase leg (CZ/CCZ/C3Z/C4Z) never targets anything (Z-type
  gates are pure diagonal on every qubit they touch, control or not), so
  window-literal-product controls may sit directly on data bits in the LEG
  for free, at any polarity, with zero ancilla cost.  Only the FORWARD (C)
  stage's RCCX/RC3X/CX gates are the ones that must never target q0-11.
- CP-CY/CP-CZ precedent: read-only reformulations of the shipped shapes are
  expensive (R1 43->73 at k=6 ancilla, ->175-181 at k=3; D1/D2 read-only
  need 7-8 ancillas with the emitter they used, exceeding a 3-ancilla cap).

=== Stage 1a: exact geometry (VERIFIED identities, cpae_core.SHAPES) ===
CP-BO's 8-piece cube decomposition of LEFT collapses, once cube-alignment
(needed only for the DATA-HOSTING architecture) is dropped: pieces
P_x0_y1+P_x0_y2+P_x0_y3+P_x1_y1+P_x1_y2(R1 part)+P_x1_y3 reassemble to
exactly R1 = rect[2,26]x[29,53] (625 px); P_x1_y2's R2t part + P_x2_y2
reassemble with P_x3_y2 to R2t=rect[27,48]x[39,43] (110px) + D1 (137px,
the true disk, not the 142px cube-clipped piece).  So the read-only
brick set only needs: R1 (1 rectangle brick), R2t (1 rectangle brick),
D1, D2 (disks).

D1 and D2 were decomposed exactly (0 mismatches vs cpae_core.SHAPES) into
disjoint rectangles by grouping rows with an IDENTICAL (xlo,xhi) pair
(script-verified, not hand-derived):
  D1 (4 groups, XOR = D1 exactly):
    x[49,61] rows [39..43]                (5 rows, contiguous)
    x[50,60] rows [37,38] u [44,45]       (2 runs)
    x[51,59] rows {36} u {46}             (2 points)
    x[53,57] rows {35} u {47}             (2 points)
  D2 (5 groups, XOR = D2 exactly; merge any 2 adjacent for a <=4-brick cut):
    x[32,48] rows [17..21]
    x[33,47] rows [15,16] u [22,23]
    x[34,46] rows [13,14] u [24,25]
    x[36,44] rows {12} u {26}
    x[38,42] rows {11} u {27}
  These match CP-CB's published nested-rectangle terms exactly.

Total brick budget under this plan: R1(1) + R2t(1) + D1(4) + D2(4, after
merging one pair) = 10 bricks -- inside the 12-brick / <=3-ancilla-per-
brick / <=4-for-D2 constraints, IF the residual (window-boundary)
computation can be made cheap.  This part was NOT reached.

=== Stage 1b: real-transpile measurement of the residual cost (VERIFIED) ===
Dyadic decomposition (cpda_lib.decompose1d) of every x/y window above
needs pieces of arity up to 6 (single-point pieces at interval boundaries,
e.g. x=2 or x=26 alone, or the D1/D2 single-row groups, fix all 6 bits).
An exact (non-relative-phase) open/negative-control X gate is the direct
way to realize such a term with a fixed polarity per bit; measured by
real transpile (u3/cx, opt2):
    k (controls)   depth   cx
        2            12     6
        3            27    14
        4            65    36
        5           130    84
        6           192   124
A SINGLE arity-4 term already exceeds the 60-layer/brick cap in this
task; arity 5-6 terms (needed at essentially every window boundary here)
are 2-3x over budget by themselves.  This rules out the naive
"open-control-X per dyadic piece, XOR-accumulate" brick design outright
and confirms (independently, by direct measurement rather than by
citing CP-CY/CP-CZ) that read-only residual computation is the expensive
part, matching the project's prior read-only-tax findings.

The intended fix (relative-phase RCCX/RC3X ladder with 2 ping-pong
scratch ancillas, echoing any needed negated data bit onto ancilla via
one X+CX pair so the ladder only ever uses positive-polarity literals)
was drafted (cpdb_probe.py) but has a wiring bug in the >3-literal branch
(scratch-wire bookkeeping) and was not debugged and verified before the
120-minute cap. No brick using it was built or measured.
