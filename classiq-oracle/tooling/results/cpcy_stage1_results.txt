NOT BUILT -- exact reasons (none of these is a proof of impossibility)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
D1, D2 at every k: not built in this task's budget.  cpcy_census.py replays
the SHIPPED blocks over all 4096 inputs and counts the peak number of live
COMPUTED values (data wires holding something other than their own raw bit
or its complement, plus non-trivial ancillas):
    R1 10, R2t 11, D1 17, D2 18   (peak, with the wires listed in the log)
That is the wire demand of a NAIVE one-to-one re-hosting of the shipped op
list.  It is NOT a lower bound on read-only feasibility: R1 and R2t are
built read-only here at 6 and even 3 wires by re-emitting from the FORM,
well under their census of 10/11.  What the census does say is that a
read-only D1/D2 needs a form-level re-derivation (D1's CP-AO form alone
carries PX,QX,PY,QY + four class codes + WX,WY,WXY + H3/HXY + KW plus the
affine preloads that the shipped block hosts in place on x0-x3,y0-y2), not
an op-list re-host, and that was outside this task's 120-minute cap.
D2 additionally FOLDS |x-40| and |y-19| onto the data wires themselves, so
its whole compute stage has no read-only analogue in the folded frame; the
fold-free frames (CP-BP..CP-BU, CP-BW) were already measured to need >= 7
wires per axis, i.e. >= 14, against 6.

k=2 for R1 and R2t: not built.  The smallest read-only forms found need
THREE wires.  R1 at k=3 already absorbs the entire y axis into the phase
(CZ + CCZ + C4Z on data-wire legs) and still needs A + hh + Wm alive at the
phase moment; pushing Wm into the phase too makes its last term an arity-4
AND with no scratch left (no relative-phase 4-control Toffoli exists at 18
wires with zero free scratch) or turns the phase legs into C5Z/C6Z, both
deeper than the k=3 build by construction.  R2t at k=3 absorbs B as
C3Z(A,U,R) and still needs A + U + R.

SEARCH BUDGETS (all work-capped, none exhaustive)
-------------------------------------------------
Control-slot local search: 300 scored candidates per (shape,k), each ranked
by a REAL transpile at opt2, seed 0, starting from identity slots.  The
reported depths are therefore upper bounds for these op lists; a larger cap
may find a few layers.  No form search was run at all -- the boolean forms
are the shipped ones (cpal_form.py, cpal_r2t_form.py) taken as given.
