=== CP-DR : one shared network per side ===================================
[S0] Setup facts (re-derived, all exact).
  The rank-10 decomposition is the GF(2) rank of F as a 64x64 matrix, so the
  x-space (col space) and y-space (row space) are FIXED 10-dim spaces; only
  the BASIS is free, and the two sides' bases must be dual.  W = span(1,
  v0..v5, atoms) has dim 17 on both sides (confirms T12).
  Consequence: CX/X preserve the span of register values, an AND adds <=1
  dim, so  min #AND per side = min length of a product chain generating W,
  and this count is INDEPENDENT of which basis of the atom space is used
  (once the span is W, any basis element is one CX away).  The two sides
  therefore decouple for AND-COUNT; only the schedule couples them.
[S1] Exact helper accounting.  Every product landing in W buys exactly one
  of the 10 dims, so  #AND = 10 + #helpers (products outside W).
  Measured: of the 2604 products of two affine 6-bit forms, 0 land in W on
  the y side (consistent with T5', MC>=3).  Closure test over all 2604
  single helpers: dim(W n L) stays 7 for every one -> >= 2 helpers ->
  >= 12 ANDs per side.  New lower bound, tighter than T12's 10.
[S2] Architecture found.  Per side the 6 data registers carry an affine basis
  T (one CX network); A = regs 3,4,5 (hi), B = regs 0,1,2 (lo).  Every 6-bit
  function is then an 8x8 GF(2) matrix and a product u(A)&w(B) is a rank-1
  matrix costing ONE AND.  Modulo F (functions of A alone + of B alone) the
  10-dim atom space is a 10-dim space of 7x7 matrices, so
     #AND terms for a side = min # rank-1 matrices spanning it.
  Because a free CX copies a live register, the atom registers form a
  spanning TREE over {0, atom_1..atom_10}, edge weight rank(M_i ^ M_j).
  Swept ~100k affine bases T per side:
    x side  MST 13, min-weight basis 12, 1-register walk 16, 2-reg 15, 3-reg 14
            best T = [20,1c,2c,06,22,21]
    y side  MST 15, min-weight basis 15, 1-register walk 17, 2-reg 16, 3-reg 15
            best T = [01,02,04,08,10,20]  (the IDENTITY split hi=v>>3, lo=v&7)
  Duality costs: the leg order is common to both sides, so a single register
  per side forces a joint Hamiltonian walk = 36 terms (x 18 + y 20);
  annealing over M in GL(10,2) (dual bases) only reached 36.
  Degree facts: every nonzero element of either atom space has degree >= 4
  (7 of degree 4, 504 of 5, 512 of 6 on the x side) so NO coset of codim <= 3
  can appear alone; a degree-6 term needs deg(u)=deg(w)=3, i.e. two scratch
  registers at that instant.
[M1] First exact 18-wire build of the shared network (cpdr_v3.prog):
  mism=0 dirty=0, AND=70 AND3=49 CX=127 legs=10, fwd2 371/556,
  BLOCK depth 804 cx 1126, wire-layers 3810, packing 0.263.
  Critical wires s3 (y sB) 512 touches, s1 (x sB) 494, s5 (y atom) 472.
  Diagnosis: only 36 of the 119 gates are atom terms; 83 are sA/sB pool
  walks (2.3 gates per term).  The pool walk, not the atom walk, is the cost.
[M2] Emitter improvements, all exact (mism=0, dirty=0, 18 wires):
  v1  naive pool rebuild per term      gates 195 (x 85 / y 110)  not measured
  v2  same, redeal undone every term   gates 119  blk2 916 / cx 1486  CX=343
  v3  persistent block basis (CX delta only)      blk2 804 / cx 1126  CX=127
  v4  pool-state-aware factor choice   gates  92  blk2 751 / cx 1004
  v5  R-light (atom register never an AND3 target)
                                       gates 137  blk2 720 / cx 1055  AND=113 AND3=24
  v6  = v5 + affine F-residues taken straight off a data register (no change)
  Sweep of 3 x-bases x 2 y-bases x 3 leg orders x 2 modes (24 exact builds):
  best 720, median ~840, worst 977.  Best = TX=[20,1c,2c,06,22,21],
  TY=identity, order [9,8,7,6,5,1,2,3,4,0], R-light.
[M3] Best measured block (cpdr_v6.prog -> cpdr_best.qasm):
  depth 720, cx 1055, forward 335/515, wire-layers 3636, packing 0.281,
  exact: check_block 0 mismatches / 0 dirty, sv_check 1.9e-14 phase, 6e-31 leak.
  Wire loads (block touches): s1 602, s3 576, s4 474, s0 434, s5 318, s2 250,
  then every DATA wire 50-118.  The six ancillas carry 2654 of 3636
  wire-layers; the twelve data wires are idle.
[M4] Falsifiers.  Step-1 falsifier (>26 ANDs on a side) is NOT triggered by
  the term count: the shared network needs only 13 (x) / 15 (y) rank-1 AND
  terms, and 18/20 on the single-register walk forced by leg duality.
  Step-2 falsifier (block over 200) IS triggered: 720.  The excess is not
  the atom network, it is the pool: 101 of the 137 gates in the best build
  are sA/sB walk gates, and they all land on two wires per side.
[S3] Step-1 tables (the shared network, one per side).
 X side, basis TX=[20,1c,2c,06,22,21], installed by a 19-CX network:
   CX x4 x5; x2 x5; x1 x5; x0 x5; x2 x3; x1 x3; x4 x3; x3 x4; x4 x3; x4 x2;
   x3 x2; x1 x2; x4 x1; x3 x1; x1 x3; x3 x1; x5 x0; x0 x5; x5 x0
   registers after it: x3,x4,x5 = A (hi), x0,x1,x2 = B (lo);
   ancillas s0 = sA (a function of A), s1 = sB (of B), s2 = the atom register.
   Shared network = 13 rank-1 AND terms (spanning tree over {0,X1..X10}),
   AND-depth 2.  Tree edges (parent -> atom : rank) 0->1:1 2->2:1 0->3:1
   0->5:1 6->6:1 0->7:1 0->8:1 0->9:1 0->10:3 1->4:2.
 Y side, basis TY = IDENTITY (hi = y>>3, lo = y&7), CX network EMPTY:
   y3,y4,y5 = A, y0,y1,y2 = B, s3 = sA, s4 = sB, s5 = atom register.
   Shared network = 15 rank-1 AND terms, AND-depth 2.  Tree edges
   0->1:1 0->8:1 0->9:1 0->10:2 0->2:2 3->4:1 0->3:2 0->5:2 6->6:1 0->7:2.
 Nesting is exploited automatically: on the y side the telescoping walk
 Y10->Y9->Y8->Y7->Y6->Y2->Y3->Y4->Y5->Y1 has hop ranks 1,2,2,2,1,2,2,2,2,4;
 the two nested chains (YL and YD) each cost 1 term for their innermost
 atom and 1-2 per level after that.
[S4] AND-depth of the whole forward is 2.  The 720 is therefore NOT an
 AND-chain latency (unlike CP-DL's 367); it is pure wire contention: two
 wires per side carry the entire pool.

CP-DR summary
1 The rank-10 decomposition's x- and y-spaces are the column/row spaces of F
  over GF(2), so they are FIXED; only the basis is free and the two sides'
  bases must be dual.  W = span(1,vars,atoms) has dim 17 per side.
2 CX/X preserve the span of register values and an AND adds <= 1 dim, so
  min #AND per side = min product-chain length generating W, and this count
  does not depend on which basis of the atom space is used: the sides
  decouple for AND count and are coupled only by the leg schedule.
3 #AND = 10 + #helpers (products outside W).  Of the 2604 products of two
  affine 6-bit forms none lands in W, and no single helper lets any product
  land in W, so >= 12 ANDs per side -- tighter than T12's 10.
4 Best shared network found: a 3+3 affine split per side makes u(A)&w(B) a
  rank-1 8x8 matrix = one AND.  Free CX copying makes the atom registers a
  spanning TREE, giving 13 terms (x, basis [20,1c,2c,06,22,21]) and 15
  terms (y, IDENTITY split hi=v>>3 lo=v&7).  Falsifier (>26/side) not hit.
5 Leg duality forces a COMMON leg order, so one atom register per side
  costs a joint Hamiltonian walk: 36 terms (x 18 + y 20); annealing over
  GL(10,2) dual bases did not beat 36.
6 Measured, all exact (check_block 0/0, sv_check 1.9e-14 / 6e-31 leak):
  v2 916, v3 804, v4 751, v5=v6 720 (cx 1055, fwd 335, packing 0.281).
  24-build sweep: best 720, median ~840, worst 977.  Falsifier (>200) HIT.
7 Cause, measured: AND-depth of the forward is 2, so this is not latency.
  101 of 137 gates are sA/sB pool walks and they land on 2 wires per side;
  s1 602 / s3 576 / s4 474 / s0 434 touches vs 50-118 on every data wire.
8 Next lever: host the pool on the idle DATA wires (a data register can
  carry a nonlinear value reversibly) to cut the max wire load ~3x.
