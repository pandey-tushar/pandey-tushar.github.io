cpen: depth attack on cpej_best.qasm (assumption -> measured result)

BASELINE RE-MEASURED
- cpej_best.qasm: 1125 gates (516 cx, 609 u3), opt2 depth 259.
  Per-wire touch counts (congestion lower bound = max): q3 142, q17 134,
  q14 123, q13 121, q16 121, q15 110, q12 105, q4 91, q11 87, q5 84,
  q10 76, q9 74, q2 74, q8 67, q6 65, q0 58, q1 56, q7 53.  LB 142.
- ASAP under the exact u3/cx commutation DAG of cpej_best.qasm: 259
  (= plain depth; no commutation slack left at gate granularity).

ASSUMPTION: the 259 is a phase-polynomial scheduling problem, so maximal
CX+diagonal regions can be re-synthesised with a depth objective.
RESULT: false at u3/cx granularity.  The 609 u3 split 303 diagonal / 306
non-diagonal (59 X-type, 247 general).  Maximal contiguous CX+diagonal
regions: 252 regions, largest 12 gates, 819 of 1125 gates covered.
No region is large enough to re-synthesise.

OP-LEVEL STRUCTURE (cpdp_best.pkl, 418 ops -> the same 270/516 circuit)
- op mix: 184 x, 108 cx, 94 ccx, 12 c3x, 12 c3x_dg, 7 cz, 1 ccz.
- cpae_core.model_depth = 240; real opt2 depth of ops_to_qc = 270.
- model critical chain 57 ops (29 ccx, 14 cx, 10 c3x/c3x_dg, 4 cz/ccz).
  Start-to-start gap histogram: 6 x15, 0 x9, 3 x8, 5 x5, 12 x4, 2 x3,
  11 x2, 9 x2, negative x5, other x5.
- The gap-6 links are "same target wire as the previous nonlinear op"
  (RCCX target profile is layers 1..7, so a target-to-target link costs 6);
  the gap-12 links are the same for RC3X (profile 1..13).  19 such links
  carry 138 of the 240 model layers.  That is the serial accumulation.
- Nonlinear same-target links: 35 are mirror undo (forced), 29 are genuine
  accumulation t ^= A then t ^= B (candidates for splitting).
- Free |0> ancillas over the op stream: 147 of 418 positions have 0 free,
  123 have 1, 35 have 2, 48 have 3, 32 have 4, 22 have 5, 11 have 6.
  Zero data wires are |0> at any point (0/418).
- Control-swap freedom: RCCX is NOT control-symmetric (swap distance 2.0)
  but every wiring occurs exactly twice or four times (43 wirings x2,
  2 wirings x4), so swapping all occurrences of a wiring cancels the
  relative phase and is exact.  RC3X is symmetric in controls 0/1 only
  (distance 0.0 for [1,0,2,3], 1.41 for [0,2,1,3] and [2,1,0,3]).
  -> 45 + 12 = 57 free binary variables; all 57 single flips verified exact.

OBJECTIVE USED FOR SEARCH (0.18 s/evaluation)
ops -> ops_to_qc -> transpile(u3/cx, opt2) -> min-atom Rz/Rx split ->
commutation DAG -> heuristic list schedule + reverse polish.
Baseline op list scores (260, 516) on this objective (cpej's CP-SAT
optimum for the same gate set is 259).

ASSUMPTION: single control swaps improve depth.
RESULT: no.  All 57 single flips exact; best raw opt2 depth 270 (13 flips
neutral, the rest 270-272); none below the 270 baseline.

ASSUMPTION: random-restart annealing over the 57 swap variables reaches
below 259.
RESULT: 3 chains x 900 s from random starts reached 262/518 (they start
above the 260 baseline; abandoned in favour of the combined move set).

=== session 2 (dependency analysis) ===
ASSUMPTION: the 259/240 is scheduling slop at op level.
RESULT: false.  Op-level dependence DAG (RAW/WAR/WAW on wires, reads
commuting) of the 418-op list has list-schedule model_depth 240 = the
baseline.  No op reordering slack at all.  DAG has 13469 conflict pairs.

ASSUMPTION: the 240 is anti-dependency from reusing the 6 ancilla wires.
RESULT: false.  Giving every ancilla EPOCH (nonzero interval, 28 epochs,
46 virtual wires) its own fresh wire: model_depth 238 (from 240).

MEASURED: value-DAG of the same op list (unlimited registers, each distinct
4096-bit value produced once; ccx 7 layers, c3x 13, cx 1):
  138 non-raw values, 8 phase ops, forward makespan 69, all phases by 72,
  ASAP peak live non-raw values 47 (mean 22.8, median 19, p90 45).
  Unlimited-register critical path of the whole op list: 82.
=> 240 / 82 = 2.9x; the gap is wire reuse under 18 registers, and the
   binding number is peak live 47 vs 6 free ancillas.

MEASURED: exact commutation of RCCX at op level: same-target RCCX/RCCX_dg
pairs do NOT commute (relative phase depends on the target), so the DAG
keeps WAW edges; disjoint-target ops commute exactly.

BANKED: cpen_search_11.pkl emitted -> cpen_best.qasm
  opt2 (258, 520), opt3 (258, 520), verify_sv18 True err 7.9e-15,
  sv_check err 3.2e-13 leak 1.7e-29.  (previous best 259 / 516.)

ASSUMPTION: a single nonlinear op can be moved off its busy target wire onto
a free ancilla (3-op form) to convert a 6-layer WAW link into a 1-layer cx.
RESULT: measured identity  RCCX(a,b,w); cx(w,t); RCCX_dg(a,b,w); cx(w,t)
== CLEAN ccx(a,b,t) exactly (and the same for RC3X/C3X); with w in |0> the
trailing cx is unnecessary.  So the move is exact when applied to EVERY
occurrence of a wiring.  Measured effect: 119 single-op candidates, best
rescheduled model_depth 240 (= baseline), worst 264; NONE improves.
1026 whole-wiring clean-host moves (57 wirings x 18 host wires): none
improves 240.

ASSUMPTION: hosting every nonlinear wiring on the critical chain at once,
each on the least-occupied wire, shortens the chain.
RESULT: false.  Round 0 hosted 24 wirings -> model_depth 614 (from 240),
nops 564, cx~829, occmax 148; round 1 -> 1387.  Writing an intermediate to
a data wire makes that write a barrier against every reader of the wire.

ASSUMPTION: detaching every nonlinear op onto its own fresh wire removes the
target-occupancy links.
RESULT: false.  136 wires, 654 ops -> model_depth 363 (from 240).
