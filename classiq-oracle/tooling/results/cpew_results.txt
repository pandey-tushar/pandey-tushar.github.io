WHAT THE SHAPING BOUGHT (measured)
  alpha = +300 on J = depth - alpha*density is the right sign and magnitude:
  it raises the share of evaluations that stay on the depth plateau from 8.3%
  (alpha = 0) to 53.3%, and it is the only setting that finds equal-depth
  states with a GROWN gate count (862 such evaluations against 0 at alpha = 0
  and 1 at alpha = -300).  Negative alpha is worse than zero on both counts
  except for plateau residency (18.8%).

WHAT BLOCKS < 249 (single measured number)
  Gate growth is absorbed but never converted into parallelism: the densest
  depth-249 state reachable grew the circuit by +47 cx and +56 u3 (520/536 ->
  567/592, density 0.3516 -> 0.3851) with the depth UNCHANGED at 249, and its
  cx per layer is 2.28 against the leader's 4.09.  Every move that grows the
  gate set further, or that hosts a whole wiring even after a 25-evaluation
  re-timing repair (best 258, +9 layers), raises the depth.  The circuit has
  632 of 1056 gates at zero slack (59.8%) and only 1.32 free-|0> wires on
  average (0 free wires at 170 of 422 op positions), so the added gates land
  in existing slack rather than shortening any critical path.
