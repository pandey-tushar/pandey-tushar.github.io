cpfl: phase-tracking / gate-moving pass on the 249 circuit (cpev_best.qasm)
=========================================================================

Question: can a CX + phase-gate view (track phases through CX like a Pauli
frame, move and cancel gates) take the verified 249 / 520 CX circuit under 150?

Facts measured
--------------
1. Walsh support of the image: 4096 of 4096.  A circuit of CX and phase
   gates only would need one rotation per parity, so nonlinear gates are
   unavoidable; the pure CX+phase form is out.

2. The transpiled 249 circuit is Clifford+T: 520 CX, 536 u3 of which 304
   are diagonal (all multiples of pi/4, 511 T-type) and 232 are H-like.

3. pyzx phase-polynomial passes (cpfl_zx.py; round trip verified exact on a
   5-wire RCCX chain, err 2e-15):
     basic_optimization      520 CX -> 520 CX, tcount 511 -> 511
     phase_block_optimize    520 CX -> 2156 CX, tcount 511 -> 509
     full_optimize (TODD)    520 CX -> 2009 CX, tcount 511 -> 509
   No phase merges exist (2 of 511) and no local CX cancellations exist.
   The block optimisers' own CX resynthesis is count-blind and quadruples CX.

4. Critical path of the 249 circuit (cpfl_crit.py):
     critical gates 632 of 1056 (335 CX, 179 diagonal, 118 H-like)
     layers with a single critical gate: 16 of 249
   The depth is a broad front of parallel chains, not one thin path.

5. Phase relocation: a diagonal phase on parity p may move to any other
   place where parity p is present on a wire.  Parity tracking over the
   whole circuit: 0 of the 179 critical diagonal phases have their parity
   present anywhere else.  Nothing can be relocated.

6. Bounds if phases were free anyway:
     all 304 diagonal phases deleted, ASAP depth        196
     CX skeleton alone (no u3 at all), ASAP depth       148
   Even with every phase gate costing nothing the circuit is 196 deep, and
   the CX dependency skeleton by itself is 148.

Conclusion
----------
Manual gate moving on the 249 circuit gains 0 layers: there is nothing to
merge, nothing to cancel, nothing to relocate, and the CX skeleton alone is
already at the 150 target.  Sub-150 requires a different circuit (fewer
serial nonlinear steps), not a rearranged one.  Phase tracking remains a
finishing tool for whatever design comes next, worth at most the diagonal
share of that design's critical path.
