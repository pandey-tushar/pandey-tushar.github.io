================ CP-DG SUMMARY (Sep 11) ================
BASIS (cpdg_basis.py, exact): D2 = XOR_{k=0..4} f_k(x) h_k(y), 0 mismatches
  / 4096.  f_k = [c_x <= 4-k] (x intervals about 40), h_k = [c_y = k] (y
  shells about 19).  dim K_x = dim K_y = 5 = GF(2) rank of the 64x64 D2
  matrix; K_x = its column space, K_y its row space; 1 not in either; ZERO
  affine elements; all 31 nonzero elements of each have degree 5 or 6.
  f_1..f_4 all contain x5&~x4; f_0 = x5 & ~(x4 & [u!=0]); h_4 = ~y5 & [v=11].
  BASIS FREEDOM VERIFIED: 3 random invertible 5x5 P, f' = P f, h' = P^-T h ->
  0 mismatches / 4096 each.  ORDER FREEDOM VERIFIED: all 120 delivery orders
  exact (legs are plain CZ on rank-1 terms, so they commute).  CP-CC's
  'B4 delivered last' blocker is an ACCUMULATOR property and does NOT apply.

EXACT THEOREM (degree, width-independent).  Every element of K_x and K_y has
  degree >= 5 over the 6 raw bits.  S_0 = span{1,x0..x5} has degree <= 1, and
  after ONE AND of arity <= 3 every element of S_1 has degree <= 3.  Each AND
  raises dim(seen) by at most 1 (CP-BV).  Hence at 6-bit width
     arity <= 3:  k >= 6 AND gates per axis;  all-RCCX: k >= 7.
  (all-RCCX: S_2 has degree <= 4 < 5.)  Both hold at ANY number of wires --
  the 6-bit analogue of CP-BZ's all-RCCX theorem.  The b6x/b6y BFS confirms
  it: level 1 is EMPTY for every k <= 5.
