STEP 3 (the 18-register squeeze; cpdm_emit18.py, cpdm_run18.py)
 method: everything is done in the BASE atom space {ONE, 12 data bits, one
 atom per AND}.  An 'acc' is not a new atom, it is a request that some
 register end up holding a given base vector, so a register's state is a
 vector and an operand set is realisable iff its vector lies in the span of
 the register states (exact Gaussian solve with the target register removed
 from the basis; the free null-space combinations are searched to minimise
 the CX count).  Constants ride along as a per-register parity and come out
 as '~' controls / X.  Two hosting tricks fall out automatically:
   * a code bit whose output XOR-set contains the raw atom d can be hosted on
     register d (accumulate S\{d}: the register then holds exactly the code
     bit and raw d is gone) -- used for h and the lev bits on y registers;
   * a dead register is cleared either by repeating its AND (unbuild) or by
     XORing its vector back from other registers.
 plus a register-pressure list scheduler, Belady eviction of the live gate
 whose next use is farthest (only if it can still be rebuilt from what is
 live), and rebuild-on-demand.

CP-DM summary
1 Depth-bounded exact synthesis works and is cheap: every piece of the
  one-comparator form has a shallow XAG.  3 lev bits at AND-depth 3 for 12
  ANDs (was 9 at depth 7); the 6 lx/m bits at AND-depth 3 for 27 ANDs (was
  23 at depth 12-17), +3 muxes = depth 4; the comparator at AND-depth 2 for
  3 ANDs.  Whole oracle 47 ANDs at AND-depth 6 (was 41 at depth ~20).
2 k = deg-1 is tight for these bits: 4 ANDs per 6-var degree-5 lev bit, 5 per
  degree-6 lx/m bit, at D <= 3.  D <= 4 bought nothing (same 27 ANDs).
3 The comparator is NOT a quadratic form: no sum of k <= 9 products of
  linear forms fits its 36 care rows (UNSAT, 2 s), so it cannot become CZ
  legs; 3 ANDs in 2 levels is the floor.
4 Cutting AND-depth 20 -> 6 did NOT cut the measured block: 367 -> 409
  (387 after term minimisation).  The cost simply moved from AND chaining to
  CX re-dealing.  A depth-bounded XAG is a WIDE XAG: 524 XOR terms over 47
  gates, 11 atoms per gate, and every operand is a |S|-long CX chain landing
  on the 12 data registers, which every gate reads.
5 At 18 registers the form does not fit at all.  The narrowest realisation
  found is 47 registers (block 1241-1341); the data registers then carry
  560 touches each and the block is 3.4x the wide one.  Minimum-AND and
  minimum-AND-depth are both the wrong objective here.
6 What would take it lower: minimise TERMS, not ANDs or AND-depth.  An XAG
  with 2-3 atom operands, even at 60-80 ANDs and AND-depth 10, would put the
  forward near the read floor.  The missing piece is a hard per-operand
  fan-in cap (|A|, |B| <= 3) inside cpdl_sat.Synth._build.
