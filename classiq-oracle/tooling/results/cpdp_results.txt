CP-DP summary
  1. Baseline reproduced exactly: R1 43 / R2t 39 / D1 71 / D2 125 = 278
     serial, merged 272/516 at width 18, sim_exact 7.8e-15, mism 0.
  2. All 12 ordered pairs have EXACT merged circuits (30-294 exact boundary
     splices each) -- the falsifier (fewer than 2 exact) does not fire.
  3. But 11 of 12 pairs are best at PLAIN CONCATENATION; only D2>D1 gains
     (193 -> 192).  Pair savings vs the serial sum: 0 to 4 layers.
  4. Full ASAP interleaving (cpdi_park.merge_asap style) breaks 27-93 of the
     inner block's gates' own products and leaves 10-18 wires dirty: the
     parking law's "re-host on a free wire" patch has no wire to use, since
     D2 dirties all 18 and D1 all but q9.
  5. Best exact full interleaving: order R2t,D1,D2,R1 with splices
     (0,0)/(11,2)/(1,19), width 18 DEPTH 270 CX 516, strict 1.156e-14,
     leak 8.2e-30, written as cpdp_best.qasm (NOT under 200).
  6. Three independent searches agree 270 is the floor for these blocks:
     720-perm descent per block under the splices (0 improvements), 11,743
     real-depth anneal evaluations with 96.5% acceptance (0 improvements),
     exact commutation-DAG ASAP (276-287, worse).
  7. Mechanism: D1 and D2 each hold ALL SIX ancillas q12..q17 non-raw for
     their whole span, so two blocks can never be live at once at width 18;
     cumulative depth 39/109/232/272 shows each block paying 97-98% of its
     solo depth.  The merged critical path is saturated (one gate per layer)
     and crosses all four blocks; the critical wire q3 is only 51% busy.
  8. What would take it lower: not a schedule, a block -- a D2 whose ancilla
     pool is <= 3 wires (leaving 3 free for a second block's tree) would let
     R1 and R2t, together 82 layers, hide inside D2's 125; that is the same
     "compliant pool" object CP-DI's P6 measured at 371 alone and is the
     only lever the machine found that is not already saturated.
