CP-DQ  phase-heavy architecture:  oracle = C ; P ; C^-1
  C builds helper values (ANDs / AND3s), P = a bag of DIAGONAL gates
  (Z free, CZ 3 layers x 2 wires, CCZ 10 x 3, C3Z 22 x 4) applied to affine
  forms of the 18 wire values.  Requirement: F = XOR over gates of the
  product of that gate's affine inputs, on all 4096 (x,y).  No shape
  decomposition, no blocks.  All numbers below are measured unless marked.

STEP 0  structure of the target (cpdq_lib.py, cpdq_sides.py, cpdq_prod.py)
  F = LOGO, 1097 pixels, rank 10 over GF(2) as a 64x64 matrix.
  10-term separable decomposition verified exact:
    F = XOR_{t in 2,4,5,6,12} [r(x)=t](x) * [|y-41|<=t](y)
      ^ XOR_{k=0..4}          [cx=k](x)   * [cy<=4-k](y)
  col(F) (x-side space) and row(F) (y-side space) are both 10-dimensional.
  DEGREE CENSUS over all 1023 nonzero elements of each (new, measured):
    col(F):  degree 4: 7 elements (a closed 3-dim subspace)
             degree 5: 504    degree 6: 512    degree <=3: NONE
    row(F):  degree 4: 15 elements (a closed 4-dim subspace)
             degree 5: 496    degree 6: 512    degree <=3: NONE
  Only one of those low-degree elements is an affine flat ({33,47,50,60},
  codim 4); the rest are not, so they are not single products of affine forms.

STEP 1a  flat covers of the 20 side functions (cpdq_cov.py)
  Minimum XOR-of-<=3-flats covers of the side functions need codim 4,5,6
  factors (Lx4 = ~x0&(x1^x2)&(x1^x3)&x4&x5 is a codim-5 flat; Lx2, Lx12,
  Ly4, Ly5, Ly6, Ly12, Dy<=4..2 have NO cover with <=3 flats at all).
  So no side function is a cheap product of affine forms of raw bits.

STEP 1b  the strict architecture (6 helpers on the 6 ancillas) is INFEASIBLE
  Exact feasibility test (cpdq_anf.py): a phase gate on k affine forms of the
  18 atoms realises a product of k affine forms, so the span of all gates with
  k <= 4 is EXACTLY the span of the 4048 monomials of degree <= 4 in the 18
  atoms.  F must lie in that span.  Measured over ~2500 helper sets
  (cpdq_t1.py, cpdq_search1.py, cpdq_s3.py: structured pool of 51 geometric /
  flat-cover / low-degree-col(F) candidates + random products of 2-3 affine
  forms over the current atoms, greedy on residual weight with restarts,
  1st- and 2nd-level (nested) helpers, x/y-separable and mixed):
    F in span:            NEVER
    best residual weight: 755 of 4096 (seed 1), 771 (seed 7)
    largest span rank:    2161 of 4096 (greedy: cd2,rd3,rd1,fx1,fx11,fy2)
    random 6-flat sets:   rank 1837-1909
  Structural reason (measured + counting):
   - no element of col(F)/row(F) has degree <= 3, so a helper built by ONE
     AND/AND3 of affine forms of the RAW bits (degree <= 3) is never a usable
     side value;
   - a degree-4 value needs two independent degree-2 helper bases plus itself
     = 3 ANDs per side (bases shared: 2 + k ANDs for k values), so 6 ANDs buy
     at most TWO side values, while the rank-10 structure needs 10 per side;
   - with only level-1 (flat) helpers every separable gate is a C3Z (px=py=2)
     and one needs 10 independent col(F) elements that are 2-form products:
     measured over 891 random flat triples the best was 3 (rank 2).
  FALSIFIER for step 1 fired here: no cover exists at all, so there is no
  "max wire load" to report for the strict variant.

STEP 1c  what the architecture DOES want:  F is two 3-bit comparisons
  (cpdq_codes.py, verified on all 4096 inputs)
    F = [bL(y) <= aL(x)]  XOR  [bD(y) <= aD(x)]
    aL(x) = level of r(x) in (-1,2,4,5,6,12) -> 0..5
    bL(y) = level of |y-41| in (2,4,5,6,12)  -> 1..5, 7 outside
    aD(x) = 5 - c(|x-40|),  bD(y) = c(|y-19|) + 1
  and for 3-bit codes u,v
    [v > u] = v2~u2 ^ ~(u2^v2) v1 ~u1 ^ ~(u2^v2) ~(u1^v1) v0 ~u0
            = CZ ^ CCZ ^ C3Z
  The two leading 1's of "1 ^ [v>u]" cancel between the halves, so
    P = 2 CZ + 2 CCZ + 2 C3Z  =  SIX diagonal gates, and nothing else.
  Cover verified EXACT against LOGO on all 4096 inputs (cpdq_cmp.py).
    gates by type:      2 CZ, 2 CCZ, 2 C3Z, 0 Z
    re-deal CX:         8 forward (2 two-term forms per comparison, hosted and
                        un-hosted)  -> 44 cx after transpile
    wire-layers:        264 (model: CZ 3x2, CCZ 10x3, C3Z 22x4, CX 1x2)
    MAX WIRE LOAD:      37   (falsifier is 110 -- does not fire, 3x under)

  Every schedule gives 51-52: the two comparisons already run fully in
  parallel (disjoint wires); the depth is the serial chain CZ -> CCZ -> C3Z

  So the PHASE STAGE OF THE WHOLE LOGO COSTS ABOUT 50 LAYERS.  Under the
  target of 200 that leaves 75 layers for C and 75 for C^-1.

STEP 2b  but the 18-wire circuit for C cannot exist (cpdq_width.py)
  P's gates use only the 12 code bits: 6 independent functions of x and 6 of
  y, all of degree 5-6, so they span 12 dimensions.  At P time the 18 wire
  values span an 18-dim space V (mod constants) which must
   (a) contain those 12 functions   -- P's forms are affine in the wires, and
   (b) SEPARATE all 4096 inputs     -- C is an invertible circuit on 18 wires
       whose six ancillas start at 0, so (x,y) -> (18 wire values) is injective.
  (a) uses 12 of the 18 dimensions, leaving 6, and those 6 must separate every
  fibre of the code map.  Measured fibre structure:
    x-classes 11, sizes 25,7,5,5,4,4,4,4,2,2,2
    y-classes 11, sizes 22,12,5,5,4,4,4,2,2,2,2
    largest fibre 25 x 22 = 550 points, needs 10 further dimensions
    => 12 + 10 = 22 WIRES.  At 18 wires no C exists, for ANY C.
  This is a clean width bound for phase-only architectures whose gates read
  class codes, and it is exactly the 18-wire wall the CP-DN/CP-DO runs hit
  from the other side.  It is the tension: the forms P needs are constant on
  the shape's classes, the wires must resolve the inputs.

STEP 3  attempts to shorten the loaded wire
  1 re-deal host swap (aD2/bL2 <-> the other member of each mixed form):
    51 instead of 52.  Recorded above (host x).
  2 C3Z -> two CCZ with an extra helper: not applicable, the C3Z's four forms
    are already 4 distinct atoms; splitting needs the e/e2 helpers, measured
    as the FLOOR row: 45 / 40 cx / max load 22.
  3 three schedules x three host choices: all 51-52 (table above).
  No further change helps: the chain is CZ->CCZ->C3Z through one wire, and
  breaking it costs ancillas that the width bound has already spent.

STEP 2c  exact replay of the EMITTED phase-stage circuit (cpdq_verify.py)
  All 18 wires initialised to the 12 code bits + six 0s, all 4096 (x,y), the
  full op list (re-deal CX, X for negative controls, CZ/CCZ/C3Z) replayed:
    every one of the 9 schedule/host variants: phase err 1.6e-15,
    wire mismatches 0  -> EXACT, depth 51-52, cx 44.
  So P is a verified circuit, not only a verified algebraic identity.

CP-DQ summary
1 F = [bL(y) <= aL(x)] XOR [bD(y) <= aD(x)] exactly: two comparisons of
  3-bit level codes (LEFT's |y-41| thresholds, D2's class sums).  Verified on
  all 4096 inputs.  This is the cheapest description of the logo found so far.
2 In this architecture the whole phase stage is SIX gates:
  2 CZ + 2 CCZ + 2 C3Z, 8 re-deal CX, no Z, no shape decomposition.
  MEASURED on 18 wires: depth 51 (opt 2 and 3), 44 cx, 264 wire-layers,
  MAX WIRE LOAD 37 (falsifier 110: does not fire), packing 0.29.
  Exact by 4096-input replay for all 9 schedule x host variants.
  Floor with three more helper values per comparison: depth 45, max load 22.
3 The strict variant of the architecture (six helpers on the six ancillas,
  twelve raw data bits) is FALSIFIED: the span of all phase gates on <= 4
  affine forms of the 18 atoms is exactly the degree-<=4 monomial span, and F
  was never in it over ~2500 searched helper sets (best residual 755/4096,
  best span rank 2161/4096).  Reason: col(F) and row(F) contain NO element of
  degree <= 3, so a one-AND helper is never a side value; a degree-4 value
  costs 2 shared bases + 1 AND, so six ANDs buy two side values where the
  rank-10 structure needs ten per side.
4 And the comparison form cannot be hosted at 18 wires, for ANY C: P's forms
  span the 12 code dimensions, leaving 6 of 18, and those 6 must separate
  every fibre of the code map -- largest fibre 25 x 22 = 550 points, needing
  10 dimensions.  12 + 10 = 22 WIRES.  That is a width bound on phase-only
  architectures that read class codes, and it is the same 18-wire wall the
  CP-DN / CP-DO value-building runs hit from the other side.
5 What would take it lower: at 22 wires this architecture is 2*C + 51, so a C
  that builds the four 3-bit codes in 70 layers gives 191 at width 22.  At
  width 18 the only way through is a phase stage whose forms are NOT class
  functions -- e.g. mixed x/y forms inside the products, which is the one part
  of the gate space this search did not exhaust.
