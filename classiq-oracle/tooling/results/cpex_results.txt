MEASURED (cpex_life.py + by hand) why not: the early uncompute of a value
needs the value's own operands back, and they are hosted ON DATA WIRES that
the nest has already overwritten.  Example, cone T1: n1 lives on wire 13 as
x3^n1 and is read only at ops 15 and 41; freeing wire 13 after op 15 requires
x3 alone on some wire, but op 15 has just replaced wire 3 by x3^n2.  The same
blocks n2, n3.  Early uncompute is therefore a RECOMPUTATION trade
(+2 RCCX-equivalents per value), i.e. objective (2) is paid for in objective
(1) currency.

NEW SEARCH VARIABLE 1 -- RELAXED DEPENDENCE DAG (cpex_rdag.py)
  ASSUMPTION: every op writes t ^= g(controls), so two writes to the same wire
  commute unless a reader sits between them; cpen_fast.dag (used by every
  search in this project) adds a WAW edge for every consecutive write pair.
  Correct relaxed model, per wire: maximal alternating groups W* R* W* R* ... ,
  every event edged from every member of the PREVIOUS group only.
  MEASURED:
    strict DAG 1133 edges.
    FULLY relaxed (nonlinear writes commute too): 1498 edges, but 0 of 60
      random schedules are exact -- permutation mismatch 0, phase error 2.0:
      the RCCX/RC3X RELATIVE PHASE does not commute with another write on its
      own target.  So nonlinear writes to a shared target must stay ordered.
    SAFE relaxation (only x/cx writes commute with each other and with
      nonlinear writes on the same target): 1664 edges, identity order EXACT
      (err 7.8e-15), objective 254 / 520 -- same as the strict identity order.

WHAT BLOCKS < 249 (single measured number, and which objective it breaks)
  It breaks objective (1) -> objective (2) coupling, and the number is:
  removing RCCX-equivalents RAISES the depth.  The one re-factoring that is
  applicable inside the 18-wire op list (n7 dissolved into CCZ, 142 -> 140
  equivalents, 520 -> 519 cx, exact err 7.8e-15) measures 257 against 254 at
  the identity order, and two independent ILS chains launched from it
  (3000 true-metric evaluations) plateau at 254 while the same searches on the
  unreduced list reach 249.  The two RCCX it deletes sit on wire 12, which is
  free at that point and therefore off the critical path.
  The other NINE verified re-factorings (which would take the DAG to 64
  forward / 128 total, floor 149.3) cannot be applied at all: each needs the
  shared value (n30, n33, m = y5*(1^y4), n9, n11) alone on a wire at the
  moment of its AND, and the ancilla budget has 152 free slots out of 1968
  (7.7%) -- 986 slots (50.1%) are idle but HELD by values whose early
  uncompute would itself cost 2 equivalents each.
  So at width 18 objective (1) and objective (2) are the same budget: every
  RCCX-equivalent removed costs an ancilla lifetime, and every ancilla
  lifetime freed costs RCCX-equivalents.
