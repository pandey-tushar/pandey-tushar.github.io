cpej -- commutation-aware depth optimisation of the shipped u3/cx circuit
input: cpdp_best.qasm; submission22.qasm is BYTE-IDENTICAL to it (diff = 0),
so there is one input, not two.  All numbers below are measured.

VERIFICATION USED EVERYWHERE
  cpae_core.check() (exact 4096-input replay) is NOT APPLICABLE to a u3/cx
  circuit: u3 is not permutation*diagonal and the simulator asserts.  Every
  circuit below is checked with cpae_core.sv_check (full 2^18 statevector on
  |+>^12 x |0>^6: max err over the 4096 diagonal entries, plus ancilla leak)
  AND by comparing the whole 2^18 statevector against the input circuit's,
  which also pins the global phase.

STAGE 1  commutation DAG over whole u3 gates (cpej_ir.py)
  exact rules used (complete for this gate set): u3 commutes with a cx iff it
  is diagonal and sits on the control, or commutes with X and sits on the
  target; cx/cx commute iff controls are equal or targets are equal.
  u3 census: 267 diagonal, 73 X-type, 243 neither.
  wire-order DAG longest path     270   (= shipped depth)
  commutation DAG longest path    256

STAGE 2  split each u3 into Rz(a) Rx(b) Rz(c) (cpej_atoms.py, exact ZXZ)
  ZXZ, not ZYZ: with ZXZ both the outer factors (Rz, commute through a cx
  control) and the middle factor (Rx, commutes through a cx target) have an
  exact commutation rule; with ZYZ the middle Ry commutes with nothing.
  1985 atoms: 1152 Rz, 316 Rx, 516 CX.  Angle census: Rz 3pi/2 x426,
  3pi/4 x234, pi/4 x231, pi/2 x185; Rx pi/2 x207, pi x83 -> Clifford+T.
  split + re-merge reproduces 270 / 516, sv err 2.8e-13.
  merge-aware longest path over atoms (a maximal 1q run on one wire costs
  one layer)                      257
  CX-only commutation longest path 54 of 516 cx  <- the 2q network itself is
  only 54 deep; the depth is set entirely by the interleaved 1q rotations.
  Global phase: the net leftover phase of a u3 decomposition is invariant
  under any redistribution of phase between gates, so it is repaired by
  inserting Rx(theta)/Rx(-theta) around one cx on its target wire (exact
  identity, absorbed by the neighbouring u3, theta found by bisection).
  Bug found and fixed while doing this: dropping an Euler angle that is
  2pi mod 4pi silently drops a factor -1 (Rz(2pi) = Rx(2pi) = -I).

STAGE 4  scheduling (cpej_sched.py, cpej_sched2.py, cpej_ils.py)
  greedy critical-path-priority layering with 1q-run merging, one pass: 262
  cx-first layering variant:                                          262

STAGE 5  exact optimum of the reordering problem (cpej_cpsat.py, cpej_opt.py)
  CP-SAT model: one integer layer per atom; precedence L_j <= L_i for two 1q
  atoms of the same wire (they merge into one u3) and L_j < L_i otherwise;
  on each wire a cx may not share a layer with any DAG-incomparable atom of
  that wire.  The model is a relaxation of the real circuit, so INFEASIBLE
  is a proof.  87569 precedence pairs reduce transitively to 2803; 1040
  layer-exclusion pairs remain.
    T = 260  FEASIBLE (1 s)
    T = 259  INFEASIBLE (1 s)
    T = 258  INFEASIBLE (1 s)
  => 260 is the exact minimum depth reachable by commutation reordering of
     the plain Rz-Rx-Rz split (see stage 6 for the form choice that beats it).
  Same run with every u3 split as Rx Rz Rx (XZX) instead: 1907 atoms,
  bound 256, exact minimum also 260 (proved).

STAGE 6  per-u3 choice of Euler form (cpej_atoms.py modes, cpej_ctx.py,
         cpej_greedy4.py)
  Four forms per u3, named by (leading, trailing) factor type; the leading
  factor is what can slide LEFT out of a merged 1q run, the trailing factor
  what can slide RIGHT:
    form 0  Rz Rx Rz        (z,z)   slides through cx controls on both sides
    form 1  Rx Rz Rx        (x,x)   slides through cx targets on both sides
    form 2  Rz Rx Rz Rx     (z,x)   control on the left, target on the right
    form 3  Rx Rz Rx Rz     (x,z)   target on the left, control on the right
  Forms 2 and 3 are 4-factor and always exist (one free parameter, fixed by
  keeping the full leading factor of the 3-factor form).  Longer alternating
  words add nothing: only the two end factors can ever leave the run.
  All four verified exact on the whole circuit (sv err 2.4e-13 .. 3.0e-13).
  Exact minimum depth (CP-SAT, proved) for uniform choices:
    all form 0  1985 atoms  bound 257  min 260
    all form 1  1907 atoms  bound 256  min 260
    all form 2  2039 atoms  bound 257  min 260
    all form 3  2357 atoms  bound 257  min 260
    context-driven per gate (cpej_ctx.py)  2012 atoms  bound 257  min 260
  What does move it: picking per gate the form with the FEWEST non-trivial
  atoms (many u3 collapse to 1 or 2 rotations in one of the four forms --
  130 gates are a single Rx in form 1, 5 are a single Rz in form 0):
    min-atom per gate   1767 atoms  bound 256  exact min 259 (proved)
  (a single gate, index 123, switched to form 1 already gives 259.)
  No single form change lowers the bound below 256 (cpej_lbsweep.py, all
  583 x 3 alternatives).  cx direction flips re-tested on the min-atom
  split (cpej_flip.py over all 516 cx): bound stays 256, no flip helps.
  Greedy at T=258 from the min-atom base (cpej_greedy4.py): all 583 gates
  x 3 alternative forms tested by CP-SAT, every one INFEASIBLE at 258
  (1674 s).  259 is a proved local optimum under single form changes.

WHY IT STOPS AT 259 -- the measured blocking structure
  Critical path of the shipped 259 circuit: 259 gates = 143 cx + 116 u3
  (input was 270 = 152 cx + 118 u3).
  The bound-achieving chain of the shipped atom DAG has 275 atoms
  (133 cx, 119 Rz, 23 Rx); its consecutive-pair reasons are
     cx then Rz on its target        111
     Rz then cx on its target        107
     Rx then cx on its control        15
     cx then Rx on its control        12
     Rz then Rx on the same wire      11
     cx then cx (control = target)    10
     Rx then Rz on the same wire       8
  chain wire visits: q17 68, q4 40, q9 38, q12 38, q3 38, q10 36, q15 32.
  218 of the 274 steps (80%) are an Rz sitting on the TARGET wire of the
  neighbouring cx.  That pair never commutes, and no splitting helps: the
  blocking factor is already a single Rz atom (T / T-dagger / S).  Each such
  segment is a phase ladder  cx(a,t) Rz cx(b,t) Rz cx(c,t) Rz ...  on one
  wire, i.e. a phase polynomial whose successive parities are all carried on
  that same wire, so those layers are intrinsically serial.
  Of the 23 Rx blockers on the chain only a handful are Rx(pi) (Pauli, and
  therefore propagatable through a cx); the rest are Rx(pi/2), the H of the
  Clifford+T form, which commutes with nothing on its wire and with no cx
  that has that wire as control.
  For comparison the cx network alone is only 54 deep under the same
  commutation rules, so 205 of the 259 layers are charged to the 1q
  rotations that pin the cx ladder in place.  Shortening the chain needs
  those phases re-hosted on other wires (parity-network re-synthesis),
  which is not a commutation move and needs free wires -- all 18 are in use.
