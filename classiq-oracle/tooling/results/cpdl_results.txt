CP-DL log (minimum-AND XAG synthesis of the one-comparator logo form, Sep 12)
files: cpdl_sat.py (exact XAG SAT synth), cpdl_pieces.py (tables + 15 affine
orbit reps of a 6-value 3-bit code), cpdl_run*.py, cpdl_emit.py, cpdl_*.prog
form: F = [lev(y) <= cap(x,b3(y))], L = lev-1 in 0..5, C = cap in 0..5.
pieces: 3 lev bits + b3 (6-var of y), 3 cap bits (7-var of x0..x5,b3) or
lx_k/m_k pairs (6-var of x) muxed by b3, comparator P (6-var, 36 care rows).
STEP 0 encodings are free up to AGL(3,2) on each 3-bit code (XOR of code bits
is free on both sides), so exactly 15 orbits of injections {0..5}->{0..7}
(20160/1344); reps in cpdl_pieces.REPS. All 3 lev bits have degree 5 in every
rep (MC >= 4 each); b3 has degree 6 (MC >= 5), ANF 12 monomials.
STEP 1 corrections/first numbers (measured, cpdl_sat.py, cadical195 forked
with a wall cap; every solution replayed on all care rows, bad=0 throughout)
 - CORRECTION: lev in 1..6, cap in 0..5, so with L = lev-1 the predicate
   [lev<=cap] is the STRICT [L < C].  All comparator numbers below use that.
 - B3 IS ALMOST FREE.  On every y with lev(y)=6 (22 of 64: y in [0,10],
   {28}, [54,63]) the predicate is 0 for every cap, so the b3 input of cap is
   a don't care there; b3 need only separate [11,27] from [29,53].
   No affine separator exists, but MC = 2:  h = 1 ^ y5 ^ (y2 & y3 & y4)
   (k=2, terms 7, AND-depth 2, 0/42 care rows wrong).  Exact b3 needs >= 4
   (k=3 proved UNSAT in 13.8 s, deg 6 => MC >= 5).  Saving: >= 3 ANDs.
 - COMPARATOR P (6-var, 36 care rows, 28 don't cares).  Swept 5 of the 15
   lev-orbits x all 15 cap-orbits (cpdl_run_cmp.py, partial table in
   cpdl_cmp_partial.txt): MC = 3 for 66 of the 75 pairs, 4 for the other 9.
   Never more.  AND-depth 2 is reachable for 17 of the 75 pairs.
 - LEV bits (6-var of y), sequential gate-sharing synthesis (cpdl_run_xy.py):
   reps 0,1,2,10 all give 3 lev bits in 9 ANDs on top of h's 2, so 11 ANDs
   for the whole y side; AND-depth 7 (rep2: 8).  terms 83/79/91/101.
 - CAP bits.  Route A (7-var of x0..x5,h, rep10): 19 ANDs [5,6,8], depth 13.
   Route B (mux, lx_k and m_k 6-var + 3 AND, rep10): 23+3 = 26 ANDs, depth
   12; rep9 26 ANDs, depth 17.  Route A is 7 ANDs cheaper, route B shallower.

CP-DL summary
1 Form F = [lev(y) <= cap(x,b3)] with L = lev-1: the predicate is the STRICT
  [L < C], L,C in 0..5.  Encodings are free only up to AGL(3,2), so there are
  exactly 15 orbits of 3-bit codes per side, not 20160 injections.
2 b3 is nearly free: it is a don't care on the 22 y with lev(y)=6, so it need
  only separate [11,27] from [29,53].  h = 1 ^ y5 ^ (y2 y3 y4), MC 2, depth 2
  (exact b3 needs >= 4; k=3 UNSAT).  Measured, 0/42 care rows wrong.
3 Comparator MC = 3 for 66 of 75 orbit pairs swept, 4 for the rest; AND-depth
  2 for 17 of 75.  Never worse than 4.
4 Piece table (n, ANDs, AND-depth): h 6,2,2 | 3 lev bits 6,9,7 (shared) or
  6,14,5 (independent) | cap 3 bits 7,19,13 | lx_k+m_k+mux 6,26,12..17.
  Whole oracle 41 ANDs vs the record's 114.3 AND-equivalents (2.8x).
5 Best measured block (exact 0/4096, registers clean): depth 367, 869 cx,
  forward 181 / 435 cx, model makespan 186, n=106, wire-layers 2080,
  packing 0.315 of 18*depth.  Best wire load only 44 touches (x1).
6 The 367 is an AND-chain latency, not width or wire contention: the x side
  alone is 26 ANDs of AND-depth 17 because sequential gate sharing chains the
  six lx/m bits.  Making the bits independent drops AND-depth to 6 but adds 9
  ANDs and measured worse (525).  What takes it lower is depth-BOUNDED exact
  synthesis (cpdl_sat.find_shallow, implemented, too slow at 15 s/profile to
  use here): 41 ANDs arranged in <= 6 levels instead of 20 would put the
  forward near 60 and the block near 130.
