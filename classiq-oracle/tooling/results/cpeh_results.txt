4. WHY 6 ANCILLAS IS THE WALL (the measured binding constraint)
   Reversible unpebbling of an ROBDD node requires BOTH its children to
   be in registers at that instant (the Margolus is undone by itself).
   - with no recomputation the whole-F ROBDD-XAG needs 36 registers
     (48 wires), measured over the best orders found;
   - with full Bennett recomputation (compute hard child, compute easy
     child, AND, uncompute both) the recursion
       P = max(3, 1+P_hard, 2+P_easy),  T = 1 + 2*T_hard + 2*T_easy
     gives, annealed over 2000 variable orders, a minimum of
       13 registers and 46085 AND executions
     (identity order: 18 registers / 577237 executions).
     46085 Margolus = 138255 CX -- 248x the board's 557.
   - the IRREVERSIBLE (black-pebble / Sethi-Ullman) number of the same
     DAG is only 5-8 (annealed minimum 5), so the entire gap is the cost
     of undoing, not of evaluating.
   - the repo's cheap XAGs are worse still: 41 ANDs needs 26 live values,
     47 needs 33, 48 needs 38 (cpdy_fit2), against 6 ancillas, and
     cpdy_fit2's rematerialisation is infeasible at 6/7/8 registers and
     costs x39 to x341 at 10-16.
   THE NUMBER: 36 registers (best whole-F XAG, no recomputation) or
   13 registers at 46085 AND executions (full recomputation), against
   the 6 clean ancillas the problem allows.  Nothing in between was
   found by the greedy reversible pebbler at any K < 38.
