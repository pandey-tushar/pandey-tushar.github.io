EXACTNESS NOTE (algebraic, verified below): |F| = 1097 is odd, so the ANF
degree-12 coefficient of F is 1.  A codim-m coset indicator is a product of
m affine forms, degree <= m.  Hence ANY XOR-of-coset-indicator representation
of F must contain an odd number (>= 1) of codim-12 terms.  A codim-12 coset
in GF(2)^12 is a single point, so >= 1 C^11Z (measured 72 depth / 60 cx) is
mandatory in this structure, and no max-codim cap below 12 can ever be exact.
