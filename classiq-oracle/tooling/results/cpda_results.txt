CP-DA stage 3: NOT RUN (time cap). Rationale recorded, not a recommendation
to stop: the k=4 tax is structural, not a scheduling artifact. Of 117
bricks, 95 have k=4 (mostly single-pixel or near-single-pixel leftover
boxes from the dyadic decomposition of non-power-of-2 rectangle edges and
disk boundaries); only 22 bricks have k<=3 (2 with k=2, 20 with k=3).
Optimal bin-packing of this k-multiset onto 6 ancillas admits at most
10 (k=3,k=3) pairs + 2 (k=4,k=2) pairs + 93 unpaired k=4 bricks = 105
rounds minimum (measured: 106-107 achieved by depth/k-sorted greedy,
close to this counting floor) -- so >=93 of 117 bricks can NEVER run
concurrently with anything, regardless of scheduling. A shared-prefix
stage (hoisting common low-bit products like x0x1, y0y1 ahead of the
bricks that use them) would need bricks to share IDENTICAL literal+
polarity sub-products; because almost all bricks are the leftover
1x1/1x2/2x2 boundary cells of a non-dyadic decomposition, their fixed-bit
patterns are largely distinct per box, so a naive prefix hoist is not
expected to close a 20x gap -- this is recorded as an open lever, not
tested, per the no-paper-only-pricing rule (not measured = not claimed).
