   WHY BQSKIT IS INEXACT HERE (measured).  Isolating the passes:
     ScanningGateRemovalPass alone, success_threshold 1e-14: 516 -> 516 cx,
       depth 259 (only 14 identity u3 dropped)  -- no cx is removable.
     ScanningGateRemovalPass alone, success_threshold 1e-8 : 516 -> 516 cx,
       depth 259 as well.
     TreeScanningGateRemovalPass (tree_depth 3, block 4), threshold 1e-14:
       516 -> 515 cx, depth 259; after exact re-timing 259 / 515, and
       verify_sv18 err 2.87e-08 / sv_check 2.11e-08.
   The stock compile() removes exactly two cx(8,17) (per-pair cx census vs
   cpej_best: cx(8,17) 12 -> 10, every other pair unchanged) and lands at
   1.40e-08.  bqskit's success_threshold is measured on a cost that is
   QUADRATIC in the amplitude error (1 - |Tr(U^dag V)|^2/d^2), so a
   threshold of 1e-14 in cost units is ~1e-7 in amplitude units; lowering
   synthesis_epsilon from 1e-12 to 1e-14 changed nothing (same 514 cx, same
   1.4e-8).  bqskit therefore cannot certify the 1e-10 amplitude accuracy
   this challenge requires, and its 258/514 is not an exact rewrite.
   Our own fitter (cpem_syn.fit3) uses a residual that is LINEAR in the
   amplitude error (least_squares on U^dag V - e^{i phi} I with an analytic
   Jacobian, Levenberg-Marquardt), and reaches 1e-15..1e-16; every rewrite
   below is accepted only at < 1e-12.

2. WINDOW CENSUS (cpem_census.py, cpem_win.py, cpem_pp.py, cpem_resyn.py)
  Definition used: a window is a wire set W plus a maximal gate-index range
  in which EVERY gate touching W lies inside W.  Such a set is convex in the
  commutation DAG (any path out of it and back must use a wire of W), so it
  can be contracted to one block and replaced.
  gate-level windows of cpej_best.qasm with >= 2 cx, |W| <= 4:  2350
    gate-count histogram 2:21 3:124 4:431 5:826 6:560 7:204 8:67 9:48 10:23
    11:11 12:11 13:8 ... largest 22 gates / 9 cx on W=(1,2,3,16)
    largest |W|=3 window 15 gates / 6 cx; largest |W|=4 19 gates / 8 cx
  wider windows (pool = each wire with its 8 heaviest cx neighbours):
    |W|=5  76 windows with >= 6 cx, largest 26 gates / 11 cx
    |W|=6 143 windows with >= 6 cx, largest 39 gates / 21 cx
    |W|=7 152 windows with >= 6 cx, largest 43 gates / 20 cx
    the large |W|=6/7 windows carry ZERO critical-path gates; the largest
    window that does is |W|=6 W=(7,8,9,10,14,17), 26 gates / 13 cx, 13 of
    them critical (|W|=7 W=(6,7,8,9,10,14,17): 28 gates / 14 cx, 13 crit).
  runs of consecutive critical-path gates and the wires they touch:
    10 gates -> 3/5/7 wires (min/med/max), 20 -> 5/7/11, 30 -> 7/9/13,
    40 -> 8/11/15, 60 -> 11/14/17.  A window big enough to hold 20 critical
    steps already spans a median of 7 wires.

3. WHY THE PHASE LADDERS CANNOT BE RE-HOSTED (cpem_pp.py, cpem_win.py)
  Split every u3 into Rz-Rx-Rz atoms: 1953 atoms = 1131 Rz + 516 cx + 306 Rx.
  Only 229 of the Rx are non-Pauli (Rx(pi/2)); the other 77 are X, which is
  inside the CNOT-dihedral group.  Removing the 229 barriers leaves ONE
  connected CNOT-dihedral region spanning all 18 wires with 477 of the 516
  cx and 1026 of the 1131 Rz -- but it is not convex, so it is not a window.
  Convex BARRIER-FREE (pure cx + Rz + X) windows, i.e. the ones a parity
  network re-synthesis could rewrite with full freedom:
    |W|=3 : 8 windows with >= 4 cx, largest 5 cx
    |W|=4 : 70 windows with >= 4 cx, largest 6 cx
    |W|=5 : 376 windows with >= 4 cx, largest 6 cx
  i.e. no phase polynomial in this circuit has more than 6 cx in a convex
  window.  Ladder census (maximal runs of cx that target one wire, separated
  only by diagonal atoms on that wire):
    wire 17: 10 ladders, 38 cx, longest 6 cx over 3 controls, 2 barrier Rx
             on those controls inside the span
    wire 12:  7 ladders, 38 cx, longest 13 cx over 4 controls, 8 barrier Rx
    wire  4:  8 ladders, 28 cx, longest  5 cx over 4 controls, 3 barrier Rx
    wire  3: 11 ladders, 36 cx, longest  4 cx over 2 controls, 1 barrier Rx
    wire  9:  3 ladders, 10 cx, longest  4 cx over 2 controls, 2 barrier Rx
  Every long ladder has non-Pauli Rx gates on its CONTROL wires inside its
  own span, so the control value changes during the ladder: the object is a
  genuine 5-7 qubit unitary, not a phase polynomial, and it cannot be
  re-expressed as a parity network over more wires.
  Idle (dirty) helper wires cannot carry phase either: a phase gadget on a
  parity p (+) d contributes (-1)^d * theta_p * (-1)^p, and cancelling the
  (-1)^d factor for all inputs forces every theta_p = 0.  A dirty wire is
  therefore only a routing element (2 cx to use and restore it).

4. CRITICAL STRUCTURE OF THE 259 CIRCUIT (cpem_lib.crit_path)
  ASAP schedule under the commutation DAG: depth 259, critical path
  137 cx + 122 u3, wire visits q17 68, q4 42, q12 34, q9 33, q10 32, q8 26.
  layer occupancy mean 4.34 gates; 15 layers hold a single gate, 49 hold
  <= 2, 69 hold >= 6.
  zero-slack gates (ASAP layer == ALAP layer, i.e. on SOME critical path):
  489 of 1125 (43%); per layer min 1, mean 1.89, max 4; 115 of the 259
  layers have exactly one zero-slack gate, 144 have two or more.
  So the critical structure is a braid, not a single chain: shortening one
  window by one layer leaves a parallel critical path in 144 of 259 layers.
