Books

Two books and one illustrated edition. The Qubit Dialogues argues about quantum computing in two voices, the illustrated edition sets the same text with engraved plates, and An Unpolarized Life is a separate, shorter book that keeps the two voices and leaves the physics out. New here? Start with The Qubit Dialogues, read online.

✦Related reading: Quantum Error Correction in Practice, four lessons.

  1. I

    The Qubit Dialogues

    A Seeker stands in front of the quantum machine with every reasonable doubt a careful person carries, and an Oracle from a few decades ahead keeps finding the assumption folded inside the doubt.

    Twenty two cantos in two voices, on superposition, entanglement, error correction and the price of the impossible. Each canto ends with a prediction, an honest measure of how much to trust it, and a question turned back at you. Where a claim is contested, the book says so in the open.

    Extent
    22 cantos · 99 pages in print
    Reading
    About two hours; about 80 minutes for the dialogue alone
    For
    Readers curious about quantum computing; the science asides add the maths

    Dialogue, with interactive figures

  2. II

    An Unpolarized Life

    The same two voices, no physics at all. A Seeker drives to a house at the end of a road carrying a question they have to answer by Friday, and by morning the question has not moved an inch and they can stand next to it.

    Twelve chapters on deciding, on what choosing costs, on being watched, on what you are entitled to keep private, on repair, and on what survives you. One object sits on the table: a square of dark glass that admits light of a single orientation and turns the rest away. The filter is a picture. The book says so out loud, in the chapter, at the time, and never claims that physics runs a human life.

    Extent
    12 chapters · 42 pages in print
    Reading
    About 35 minutes
    For
    Anyone facing a decision; no physics at all
    Form
    Dialogue, text only, readable in a sitting

  3. III

    The Qubit Dialogues, Illustrated

    The same cantos cut as engraved plates, each one drawn from a shared vocabulary of motifs and moved by nothing but your own scroll.

    All twenty two cantos are composed, each one drawn from a shared vocabulary of motifs so the volume reads as one press rather than twenty two separate pictures. Every plate is seeded, so it renders the same on every machine and every visit. The prose is the finished prose from the main edition.

    Extent
    22 cantos · 96 pages in print
    Reading
    About two hours, the same text as the main edition
    For
    Readers who want the cantos with the art, or a second reading
    Form
    Generative artwork, drawn live in the browser
