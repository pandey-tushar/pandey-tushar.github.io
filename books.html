Catalogue of editions

Books

Three editions, all of them the same argument held at a different angle: that the thing you were told is settled is usually a true statement about one machine, mistaken for a law about all of them.

✦Every edition reads in the browser and downloads as a PDF. Nothing is behind a form, a login or a price.

  1. I

    The Qubit Dialogues

    A Seeker stands in front of the quantum machine with every reasonable doubt a careful person carries, and an Oracle from a few decades ahead keeps finding the assumption folded inside the doubt.

    Twenty two cantos in two voices, on superposition, entanglement, error correction and the price of the impossible. Each canto ends with a prediction, an honest measure of how much to trust it, and a question turned back at you. Where a claim is contested, the book says so in the open.

  2. II

    An Unpolarized Life

    The same two voices, no physics at all. A Seeker drives to a house at the end of a road carrying a question they have to answer by Friday, and by morning the question has not moved an inch and they can stand next to it.

    Twelve chapters on deciding, on what choosing costs, on being watched, on what you are entitled to keep private, on repair, and on what survives you. One object sits on the table: a square of dark glass that admits light of a single orientation and turns the rest away. The filter is a picture. The book says so out loud, in the chapter, at the time, and never claims that physics runs a human life.

  3. III

    The Qubit Dialogues, Illustrated

    The same cantos cut as engraved plates, each one drawn from a shared vocabulary of motifs and moved by nothing but your own scroll.

Every plate is seeded, so it renders the same on every machine and every visit. The prose is the finished prose from the main edition.
