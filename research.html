Number theory

Primes, twin primes and Goldbach.

  1. 15

    An intermediate conjecture between Goldbach and Dubner: every even number is the sum of a prime and a twin prime

    Tushar Pandey, sole author

    Studies the conjecture, proves a conditional positive-density result, and verifies it numerically up to 1014.

    The conjecture itself is not proved. The density theorem assumes a lower bound of Hardy-Littlewood order for twin primes; the statement is checked by computer for every even number from 6 to 1014.

    Venue
