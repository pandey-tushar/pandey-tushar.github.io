Interactive lessons

Quantum error correction in practice

Four interactive lessons, from a single surface-code patch to teleporting a T state between logical qubits.

They are for readers who know what a qubit is and have met the H, T and CNOT gates; the checks, the logical operators and the patches are introduced as they appear. Each lesson is one page of five to eleven steps that you play or step through, with every step also written out in full; later lessons reuse the earlier ones, so take them in order.

All four use the smallest surface code, nine qubits at distance 3. Tushar Pandey's own work in quantum error correction goes further, from an error-correction algorithm for the doubled semion model to surgery on qLDPC codes: see his research.

  1. Lesson 1A surface code patchEncoding |0⟩L, the eight checks, and what a single error does to them.
  2. Lesson 2Logical CNOT between two patchesNine physical CNOTs between two patches make one logical CNOT and a Bell pair. Run it live in the browser.
  3. Lesson 3Preparing a T stateH and T on one qubit, then encoding: the resource state for a non-Clifford gate.
  4. Lesson 4Teleporting the T stateFrom patch A to patch C through a logical Bell pair on B and C.
