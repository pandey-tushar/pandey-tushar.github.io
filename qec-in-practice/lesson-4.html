Lesson 4: Teleporting the T state

Patch A holds |T⟩​L (lesson 3), B and C become a logical Bell pair (lesson 2), then a logical CNOT A to B and the readouts of A in X and B in Z teleport the T state to C, up to a Pauli fix applied to the measured bits.

Step by step

  1. prepare A. H on q0 of A, as in lesson 3.
  2. prepare A. T on q0 of A: the T state.
  3. encode A. Encode A: |T⟩​L.
  4. prepare B. H on q0 of B, as in lesson 2.
  5. encode B. Encode B: |+⟩​L.
  6. encode C. Encode C: |0⟩​L.
  7. transversal CNOT B→C. The logical CNOT from lesson 2, B → C: B and C become a Bell pair.
  8. transversal CNOT A→B. Logical CNOT A → B ties the T state on A to the Bell pair.
  9. readout rotation A (X). H on all nine qubits of A swaps X and Z: the checks swap type, and all eight still hold. A is read in X.
  10. readout rotation C (X). H on all nine qubits of C swaps X and Z: the checks swap type, and all eight still hold. C is read in X, to check that it now holds the T state.
  11. measure. Every qubit is measured in Z. Only the checks that are Z parities can still be read. The X bit of A and the Z bit of B say which Pauli fix C needs.
